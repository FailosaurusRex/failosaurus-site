# Failosaurus Rex logo system

**Concept: the F with the tiny arm.** The mark is a bold geometric "F" that is also a T. rex in profile: the top bar is the head (eye, one crooked tooth), the stem is the body, and the F's middle bar is shrunk to the famous tiny arm, drawn in electric green. The whole thing is tilted 7 degrees, because it never quite stood up straight.

**Why it works.** One idea, three reads: a letter, a rex, a joke. It is built from rectangles and one curve, so it survives 16px (the head and the green arm still read). The flaw is deliberate and warm: honest about falling short, not sloppy. The green arm is the punchline and the only accent.

Explored: boxy X-eyed head (read as a gun), FRX with a fallen X, stacked "attempts" F, blocky dino body (read as an elephant). See `explore/contact-sheet.png`.

**Usage rules**
- Dark tile mark is the default; on dark pages the tile blends in and the F floats. On cream use the `-on-light` lockups.
- Colors only: #110c08, #ede5d8, #39ff14. The arm is always green (or the same color in mono versions).
- Never straighten the tilt, add gradients, shadows, or a cartoon dinosaur.
- Clear space: one arm-width (about 1/8 of the mark) on all sides. Minimum size: 16px mark, 120px wordmark.
- Use `favicon.svg` below 32px, `avatar.svg` for round crops.
